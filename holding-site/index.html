About Khilco Holdings

A holding company built to launch, own, and scale operating businesses

Khilco Holdings, LLC sits above our portfolio of operating companies. Rather than running a single product line, we identify, build, and support ventures across technology, operations, consumer, and service categories — providing shared infrastructure, capital allocation, and operating discipline so each venture can focus on its own market.

We are early-stage and self-funded to date. We're now raising growth capital to accelerate our first operating venture and fund the next additions to the portfolio.

Build

We incubate new ventures from the ground up when we see a gap worth building for.

Own

We take direct, long-term ownership stakes rather than passive minority positions.

Support

Portfolio companies share back-office, technical, and operating resources through the holding company.
